# What kind of stats are these?

Live, tick-accurate board through the current playhead: K/D/A, ADR, KAST, rating, entry, money, and CT/T splits.

Rating is a proprietary **1.00–10.00+** scale (about **5.25** for a typical game) developed by whiskeyo. The [rating page](/rating) has the formulas and ranges.

## ADR and trades

ADR is enemy health damage capped at remaining HP. Trades use a 5-second window.

## Sides and overtime

Side swaps and overtime follow competitive scoring, not a raw CT-vs-T round count.
