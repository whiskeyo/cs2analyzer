# GOTV or POV? What is the difference?

GOTV is the only supported demo type. GOTV demos are the server-native demos that record the whole gameplay - they are widely used on FACEIT, HLTV demos, and even Premier games.

POV demos, i.e. demos recorded by players with `record <demo_name>` are not supported. Valve handles them a bit different, so POV-based demo can be troublesome.
