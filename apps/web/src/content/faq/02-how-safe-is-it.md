# How safe is it?

This application lives only in your browser, which has its own advantages and disadvantages. 

The best thing is that your data is as safe as you are - since nothing is uploaded to any external servers, your analyses and playbooks stay on your computer. You can share your stuff with friends or teammates by exporting & importing data explicitly, and nobody can access the data unless you share your JSONs with them.

Safety comes at a price though. You need to take care about your notes - backup your data frequently, since if your PC dies, your notes die too. At least, noone can break into your account and steal your stuff, right?
