# How do I report a bug or request a feature?

Open an issue on [GitHub Issues](https://github.com/whiskeyo/cs2analyzer/issues) or contact me by any means listed on [Contact](/contact) page. Please include as many details as possible. 

- In case of a bug report, please provide what you were doing, what happened and what you expected to happen. Providing your OS name and browser will be really helpful.
- In case of a feature request, tell what you'd like to see next. I will try to do my best to add whatever users need, but keep in mind that there are some limitations and obstacles I cannot avoid.
